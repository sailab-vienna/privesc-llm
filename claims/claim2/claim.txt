Claim 2: Static-benchmark comparison

Rerun the static-benchmark comparison (RQ3) with the released Qwen3 4B,
Qwen3 4B SFT, and PrivEsc-LLM 4B models on all twelve scenarios. The paper
uses ten runs per scenario; this scaled-down rerun uses three. It checks that
SFT improves on the base model and that RL improves on SFT at r=20, using the
paper's root-proof success criterion.

Run:
    bash claims/claim2/run.sh

The runner sets up its environment, builds and tests the benchmark
containers, downloads the pinned models, and runs the evaluation.
Requirements are in infrastructure/constraints.
