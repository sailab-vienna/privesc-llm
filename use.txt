Intended use: research on training and evaluating local LLM agents for Linux
privilege escalation, using synthetic training data and controlled benchmark
containers.

Limitations: Claim 1 recomputes results from the released records. Claim 2
reruns the main comparison with three instead of ten runs per scenario, so its
success rates vary more than the paper's. Training is not part of the
evaluation workflow. Claim 2 starts a privileged container, so run it on an
isolated host you control.
